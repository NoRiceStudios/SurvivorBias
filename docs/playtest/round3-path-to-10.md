# Round 3: what would make the game a 10 for fun?

Both testers played one more theater of the current build (with visible strategic bombing, the stalemate rule and the stores economy). Then each answered one question: what would the game need for a fun rating of 10/10? Each gave at least three recommendations.

## Marta Kowalczyk (strategy veteran)

*Played Seasoned (seed `marta5`) for 17 weeks.*

- **Narrow Sea:** DEFEAT after 10 weeks; 22 aircraft lost, 97 enemy kills claimed, one sector lost to the enemy.
- **Kessel Basin:** stopped in week 8 of 10, with the front at −5.

**Current fun rating: 7/10** (round 1: 6, round 2: 7).
- **Up:** for the first time the loss felt earned, and the enemy's bombing hurt (2 aircraft grounded every week from W6 to W10).
- **Down:** the front stayed opaque, and there was no way to answer the damage.

**What it takes to get to 10**

1. **Put numbers on the front. [small tweak]**
   - The liaison says only "in our favour" or "strongly against us".
   - Kessel W16 ended at +23, one point short of a sector; W17 fell to −5 with only "close support: strongly against us" as explanation.
   - Wants a numbered ledger in the debrief and an expected range on the plan screen.
2. **Let me answer the enemy's strategy. [big change]**
   - Her airfields stayed at 50% for 5 weeks and could not be repaired.
   - Meanwhile 396 supplies sat unspent.
   - Wants paid repairs to works, a visible job for radar and flak, and intel warnings like "bombers massing at Hafenburg".
3. **Make deep strikes pay off, and let me prove it. [big change]**
   - Two escorted strikes grounded only an estimated ~8% of the enemy's operations.
   - Her recon plane had no crew from W11 until about W17.
   - Wants a clear tipping point (for example, "below 50%, their fighter cover halves") and a crew with the first recon aircraft.
4. **Let the armor lesson change during the war. [big change]**
   - Deadly zones should shift when the enemy brings in new guns, announced only through radio calls and Operational Research.
   - Wants a running count of crews saved by plate for each zone.
   - Right now the puzzle is solved once by week 5.
5. **Squadron leaders worth caring about. [small tweak]**
   - She lost 12 leaders in 17 weeks, and surnames came back.
   - Leaders should survive more often and gain a trait after about 5 operations.
6. **Make orders and requests consistent. [small tweak]**
   - A strike order was re-issued right after it was fulfilled.
   - A squadron at 0/10 fatigue asked for a stand-down.

*Summary:* "The war can now be lost, which is good. To reach 10 I need to see the front's numbers, be able to answer the enemy's bombing, and get a payoff from deep strikes I can prove, with an armor lesson that keeps changing."

## Jonas Berg (narrative player)

*Played Green (seed `jonas5`) for 15 weeks.*

- **Narrow Sea:** decisive DEFEAT in 7 weeks, 14 aircraft lost.
- **Kessel Basin:** 4 more weeks played.

**Current fun rating: 7/10** (unchanged).
- **Up:** the armor feedback works ("The engines plate on JT-236 is dented deep…"), and last words shown next to names are strong.
- **Down:** leaders die too fast to care about, the front collapsed without warning, and stores shortages felt like arithmetic.

**What it takes to get to 10**

1. **Leaders who live long enough to become characters. [big change]**
   - He lost 8 leaders in 11 weeks.
   - Hale asked three times for an offensive role, got it in week 9 and died, with the same one-line notice as anyone.
   - Wants leaders who survive more often, an obituary or letter when a veteran dies, and a memory of what was approved or declined for him.
2. **Give quiet weeks and theater changes some human content. [big change]**
   - A rest week showed "0 of 0 aircraft returned" and nothing else.
   - Wants letters home, funerals, new pilots arriving, and prisoner-of-war postcards for the men whose chutes were counted.
3. **Warn me before the front breaks. [small tweak]**
   - Wants a line such as "The line at Saltcote is cracking" one week ahead.
4. **Fix the text that breaks the spell. [small tweak]**
   - Replacement leaders took the surname of the man who just died.
   - Leaders repeated the same remark five times, and a fighter-sweep leader complained about covering bombers.
   - The radio said "I count 4 chutes", but the Missing list showed "Last heard: nothing".
   - A sortie order was issued already due this week.
5. **Make stores shortages a choice, not a puzzle. [small tweak]**
   - "Fit to stores" dropped the escort first and sent 3 Harrows out alone.
   - Wants a warning for unescorted bombers, bombers and escorts dropped together, and human-sized choices such as "short rations: morale −1".
6. **Make Green's opening match its description. [small tweak]**
   - He lost 9 aircraft and 4 leaders in weeks 1–3, although the briefing promised light losses.

*Summary:* "The game now shows me that my choices saved crews. To reach 10 it has to let a few of those crews live long enough to become people, and give their deaths, the quiet weeks and the lost wars a moment of their own."

## What was done straight away (small tweaks)

- **Leaders:**
  - When a leader's aircraft is lost, about half the time he bales out and makes it back.
  - Leaders also nurse damaged aircraft home more often.
  - No name used in the war is reused, and the officer name pool is four times larger.
- **Text:**
  - Leaders don't repeat a remark two debriefs running, and there are more remarks.
  - The remarks fit the mission (a sweep is not about covering bombers).
  - The Missing list now shows what squadron mates saw ("Able 3 reported it 'falling out of formation', 4 chutes seen").
- **Front warning:** the Army warns one week ahead when either side's line is about to break.
- **Orders:**
  - Sortie quotas run over two weeks.
  - A site struck to order is not ordered again straight away.
- **Requests:** stale stand-down requests are dropped when the wing redeploys.
- **Stores:**
  - "Fit to stores" drops bomber squadrons together with a matching escort.
  - The adjutant warns when bombers will fly unescorted.
- **Green:** the enemy flies smaller operations for the first three weeks.
- **Crews:** squadrons with no crew at all (such as a new recon flight) get the first graduates.

The big changes go to the designer for a decision.

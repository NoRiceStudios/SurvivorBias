# Jonas's journal

## Campaign 1 — green, seed jonas1

**Wk 1–2.** Title screen is gorgeous, the quote "Armor the places where the returning aircraft were not hit" sticks with me. Briefing paper look is lovely. Defaults were "strike the airfield", that matched High Command's order so I just launched. Lost 4 Kestrels in week 1 (2 Vespers, 2 Saints at home) — the "Skipper's hit— I can't hold her— who can fly—" line got me. But the Missing list is just serial numbers and "crew 1": no names. I want names.
Funded VHF radios (hear the last calls of lost crews — that's what I care about). Ordered 2 Kestrels.
Confused: top bar icons have no labels (what is 160 vs 140 vs 90?). Bottom bar "Kesselspitze Airfield: 17 aircraft · Defence 6 fighters" — is that them or us? Intel says enemy fighters ~21, the Air Ministry letter says ~11. Which?
Week 2 forecast "Storms" but the raid log never mentioned weather. Gloomy Mabey said "~0 fighters" and also "there were more of them than last time". Raids hit the airfield 2 weeks running but army pressure went from +10 to -5 — I don't understand what moves the front.

**Wk 3–5.** Switched to Close Support in wk 3 because pressure turned against us — Kesselspitze taken! Best moment so far. Wk 4 was awful: 6 lost incl. a Harrow with 5 men. Three radio calls "We're burning, the tanks are going, get out—". The ground crew keeps saying "more plate on the outer wings/fuselage", but the planes that died were burning from the tanks. The title quote clicked: armor where the RETURNING planes were NOT hit. Moved bomber plates from outer wings/tail to fuel tanks (2) and engines (1). Hangar line "The pattern is clear enough. The question is what it means." — lovely.
Wk 4 "Destroy 12 enemy aircraft by week 4" — I already had 13 claimed when it was issued, then it said NOT fulfilled. Apparently it counts only that week? Unclear.
Wk 5: noticed by accident that every squadron is at fatigue 9/10. Nobody warned me. Rested Vespers. Lost 2 more Harrows (10 men). Factory only makes ~1 plane/week; I have 28 spare aircrew and nothing for them to fly.

**Wk 6–10.** Rest week (wk 6) still cost me 2 Vespers on defence and the front slid from +16 to +1. Close support wk 7–10: lost 5, 2, 2, 3. Radio calls now are "Both engines gone", "Tail's shot away", "Controls are gone". I moved plates to engines/fuel/tail. Hard to tell if it did anything — I kept losing planes either way. No feedback that says "armor stopped a hit".
One Harrow of the Ploughmen (Lisle, "Showman") claimed 5 kills in wk 9. Ha. Wk 9 Air Ministry: "enemy fighter strength assessed as broken" — the same week 14 fighters jumped us. I think HQ believes our claims. That's the theme, I see it.
Wk 10: The Narrow Sea VICTORY — but by "holding the advantage", only +1 sector. No celebration screen, just one line, and I'm straight into the Kessel Basin with 11 aircraft out of 28.
Fuel (594) and munitions (352) pile up and never matter. Supplies are the only thing that counts. 52 spare aircrew with no planes.
**Wk 11.** Ploughmen wiped out: "NO REPORT — 0/3 returned." Two of them "Engine fire! Extinguisher's useless—" with 2 plates on the engines. Lisle just... vanished; next week a "Flt Lt Roland Pryce" leads the Ploughmen with the SAME "Showman" trait. No word about Lisle at all. That hurt more than the game seemed to notice.
Bug-ish: squadron note reads "Turn survivors of the No. 73 "Saints" are badly shaken." (typo, and printed twice for Vespers).

**Wk 12–20 (Kessel Basin).** Two rest weeks to rebuild — factory makes ~1 plane a week while the enemy bombs it. Kestrel fighters (single engine, crew of 1) radio "Both engines gone, we're losing height—" and "Number two is burning, can't feather it" — that's a bomber line on a fighter. Bug.
Eisenfeld taken wk 18 with only 6 aircraft flying; earlier +32 pressure took nothing. I don't understand when pressure turns into a sector.
Order in wk 19 "...Kesselstadt in our hands, by week 21" — but the theater ended in week 20.
Wk 20 Basin VICTORY again "by advantage". First recon Swift lost on its first sortie ("photo P9" errored because I gave it before assigning the squadron; the plane flew somewhere anyway).
**Wk 21–25 (Northern Approaches).** Nightjars wiped out (wk 21), Mabey gone without a word. "The wing's folding— it's coming off at the root—" → put a plate on wing roots. I've stopped believing the armor does anything; bombers with 2 plates on engines keep dying of engine fires.
BUG: the first heavy bomber squadron was formed as No. 15 "Vespers" — the same name as my fighter squadron. Debrief then shows two "No. 15 Vespers" rows, and the radio has two "Vesper 1"s. The Colossus (crew of 9!) died on its first sortie.
BUG: names recycle — S5 leader "Wg Cdr Leonard Hale" while S1 is led by "Flt Lt Leonard Hale"; S3's new leader "Desmond Hale"; S4's "Roland Pryce" next to "Douglas Pryce".
Duplicate standing order: "Support the Army until Nordwall is in our hands" by week 26 AND by week 27.
"Saint 1: Bomb doors open. Hold her level." — a fighter, after the only bomber was shot down.
119 spare aircrew, 1,500 fuel, 900 munitions. Only supplies matter.

**Wk 26–27: the end.** Second Colossus (9 men) gone on its first sortie. Switched to a fighter sweep in wk 27 — 4 Kestrels lost, one written off on landing, and the Northern Approaches broke: "THE FRONT HAS COLLAPSED". War over in week 27.
Result: 58 aircraft lost. Crews claimed 81, actually destroyed 34.
The DECLASSIFIED tab is the best screen in the game: three bombers side by side ("What you saw: 452 holes", "What you never saw: 243 holes", "The 25 hits that brought them down") plus a chart of how likely one hit in each zone is to bring a plane down: cockpit 32%, fuel 26%, engines 24%, wing roots 12%, nose 10%, outer wings 0%, fuselage 1%. I had cockpit at 1 plate and the nose at 0 the whole war, and those were the zones with the FEWEST holes in my hangar survey (nose 0%, cockpit 2%). I half-got it: I followed the radio's last calls (fuel/engines), not the empty spots. Gut punch, and a good one.
Afterwards the briefing still shows "theater week 8/10", new orders "by week 29", and pressure "+2 in our favour" after the collapse. Leftover state.

## Campaign 2 — seasoned, seed jonas2
Plan from what I learned: armor the zones with the FEWEST holes (nose, cockpit, engines, fuel), stop listening to the Chief Fitter. Get Gun Cameras + Intelligence Section early. Rest squadrons before fatigue hits 9. Don't burn the wing on close support every week.
**C2 wk 1–5.** Re-armoring all four squadrons cost me 120 of my 160 supplies (4 per plate moved, and removing a plate counts too). Nothing warned me before it was spent.
Even with nose/cockpit/engines/fuel armored, wk 4 close support cost 6 (3 Harrows). Kesselspitze fell anyway.
Intelligence Section line is great: "estimates of enemy fighters range from 0 to 11. At least one squadron is badly wrong." But the same report shows Nightjars "Enemy fighters: none seen · Attacks came mostly: from astern".
Wk 5: tired of "Order NOT fulfilled" for kill quotas I can't meet. Switched returns to OPTIMISTIC, flew a 6-plane sweep. One "Splash one bandit" on the radio → Vespers claim 7. HQ: "Order fulfilled — noted with satisfaction", a commendation, confidence 78→91. I felt like a fraud. It's the point of the game, and it works on me.
**C2 wk 7–8.** Caught: "Air Ministry photographic interpretation does not support your claims. A formal inquiry has been opened." plus "Form 1180 ... in triplicate". Confidence 96→81. Embarrassing, and I loved it. Went back to accurate. Narrow Sea broke in week 8 (decisive).
**C2 wk 9–13.** Took Eisenfeld (wk 10). Rested in storms, then took Kesselstadt in wk 13 ("Secondary objective achieved", +120 supplies). Basin won in 5 weeks. Storm forecasts never seem to change anything I can see; one storm week the log said "Sky is clear. No fighters yet."
Same duplicate-name BUG: the recon squadron was formed as No. 61 "Long Odds" — same as my Harrow squadron. Stale orders from the previous theater still turn up in Correspondence (Brandmoor in wk 9; two different "Destroy N" orders in wk 14, only one listed under Standing orders).
**C2 wk 14.** Northern Approaches: ONE close-support raid took Nordwall AND Grauburg. "THE WAR IS OVER: VICTORY" after 14 weeks. Anticlimax: the final theater, the one with spring offensives and the "Last reserves" stage, lasted a single turn. 18 lost, 56 claimed (61 reported), 27 actually destroyed.
Overall: seasoned was far easier than green for me, because by then I knew the trick. Green, played the way the game nudges you, gave a death spiral I couldn't climb out of.

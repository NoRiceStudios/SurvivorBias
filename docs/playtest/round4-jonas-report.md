- *Narrow Sea: VICTORY in week 6, 14 aircraft lost.*
- *Kessel Basin: VICTORY in theater week 9, 22 aircraft lost.*
- *Northern Approaches: 5 weeks played, 13 aircraft lost.*

## 2. What improved / what still bugs me

**Improved**
- Obituaries remember my decisions, and the Red Cross post brings men back.
- Reputations give leaders shape: Ridley "Steady", Brackley "Ace", Penrose and Carrow "Shaken".
- VHF last words hit hard.
- The Army found an airfield "in working order" and my claim was withdrawn. That is the game's theme in one line.
- Front warnings work.
- These are fixed:
  - stable request numbers;
  - kill quotas that scale with the wing;
  - the Missing list matches the radio.

**Still bugs me**
- **Leaders now die too rarely, and "missing" has stopped meaning anything.**
  - Hale, Penrose and Lisle each got an obituary, then turned out to be prisoners. By the third time I stopped believing the letters.
  - Hale, Penrose, Carrow and Sallow baled out and walked back.
  - Then in week 18 I lost three leaders in one operation.
- **Obituaries read like a ledger.**
- **Quiet weeks are still empty.**
- **Theater changes are one line.**
- **Victory comes too fast.** The wing was gutted but never in danger.
- **An impossible order:** a strike only heavy bombers could reach, and I had none.

## 3. What it takes to get to 9 or 10

1. **Make "missing" a real waiting period. [small]**
   - First a telegram, then weeks of waiting, then the Red Cross, an evasion or "presumed killed". The letter goes out only then.
   - Survival should stay at about 1 in 4.
2. **One human detail per death. [small]**
   - His last radio call, his best remark, a line from his successor.
   - Mention only the one decision that mattered.
3. **Theater interludes and quiet-week scenes. [big]**
4. **Make losses cost something in the war. [big]**
   - Confidence should fall with losses.
   - A thin wing should risk a stalemate.
5. **Give leaders faces. [small]**
6. **Check that remarks match the facts. [small]**

## 4. Bugs and text that break the spell

- "Easy. Too easy, really." after the leader's whole crew died.
- The radio said "No chutes", yet the leader walked back.
- "his crew is missing" on a single-seat fighter.
- "NO REPORT" while the leader had walked back.
- "Heavily outnumbered" with about one fighter seen.
- "Close Support with none… Orders are valid".
- A one-aircraft squadron flew with no warning.
- Request records were dated a week early.
- Grammar:
  - "1 of the crew … are"
  - "1 crews"
  - "1 operations"
  - "as glory-seeker"
  - "all Ferrys"
- A Wg Cdr succeeded a Flt Lt.
- Surnames repeat within a war, and officer surnames are the same in every campaign.
- Cautious leaders asked to "press our attacks home".
- The morale notes were duplicated.

# Marta's journal — Survivor Bias

## Campaign 1 (seasoned, seed marta1)
**W1-3.** Default plan struck Kesselspitze AF. Crews claimed 39%/58% destroyed -> map says "believed 3%". Secondary objective "achieved (on crews' reports)". Smells of overclaiming — good, that's the theme.
W2: applied Wald: moved all bomber plates off outer wings/fuselage (39%/26% of holes) onto nose/cockpit/engines (0%/0%/13%). Armor moves didn't seem to cost the 4 supplies/plate the hangar promises (159 -> 79 only after 80 research). Bug?
W3: catastrophe — 5 of 10 Harrows lost on the same airfield, "Nothing heard" x4. No way to tell whether my armor change helped or hurt. HQ now orders 17% damage on an airfield it "believes" at 6%, and both orders failed. Intel bulletin W4: "enemy fighter strength assessed as broken" — that's my own inflated claims coming back at me. Lovely, but I'm bleeding.
UI line in debrief: "High Command judges you on the returns you send, not on what happened." -> optimistic returns look like an exploit to test.
Text oddity: S1 on home defence still says "The flak was the worst I have seen" / "recommends the target be given to the heavies".
**W4-10.** W4 fighter sweep, 0 losses. W5 switched to OPTIMISTIC returns as a test; HQ immediately withdrew the W1 airfield claim from its own photos ("DISCREDITED"). Nice — the lie catches up.
SUSPECTED BUG: "research photoRecon" in W2 took 80 supplies, but at W6 research screen said "Engineers are idle" and Photo Recon unresearched, no notice. Paid 80 again. Lost 80 supplies.
W6-9 close support with tight formation (0.9): W7 15/15 back and pressure jumped to +26; W8 Army took Kesselspitze. Close support is clearly the lever that wins theaters; deep strikes just feed bombers to flak for numbers I can't verify.
W9 first photo: Hafenburg Assembly Plant at 100% — so my W2 raid did nothing (crews said "unobserved").
W10: VICTORY on advantage (+1 sector of 2). Then "formal inquiry" for my optimistic returns. Confidence reset to 60 anyway at theater change, so what did the lying cost me? Unclear.
Odd: orders "by week 11/12" issued in a 10-week theater; "13 aircraft by week 7" failed then reissued as "13 by week 8" (due same week). Saint 4 aborted at T+008 with hydraulics, then "Saint 4 is going down" at T+053 over target.
Fighter fatigue hits 9-10/10 within 3 weeks even on home defence, but I never see what fatigue actually does.
**W11-19 (Kessel Basin).** Close support + escort every week. W11 the recon Swift was lost; W12 order said "Recon: S5 (0) photographing..." with ZERO aircraft, still "Photo-reconnaissance aircraft failed to return" -> bug.
W13 VHF radios: now the missing crews talk. "Fuel's pouring out of the wing", "We're burning, the tanks are going", "Main spar's gone" (wing root), "Number two is burning" (engine). Fuel tanks at 3% of survivor holes = classic Wald. Moved plates to fuel 3 / engines 2 / cockpit 1. This is the best moment of the game so far — the research literally gives you the bomber-holes data that's missing.
W16 FEINT over sector 5: "Sky is clear. No fighters yet." 13/13 back, Eisenfeld taken. Feint + close support is a dominant combo; W19 Kesselstadt taken, secondary objective "achieved and confirmed" just by the Army walking over it. Never bombed the works deliberately.
Kill-quota orders ("Destroy 13/16/15/18 enemy aircraft") almost always fail; ~3 claims per week is the max with gun cameras. W20 gave me two kill quotas at once due the same week (18 and 13 by W21).
Text bug: squadron note "Turn survivors of the No. 73 "Saints" are badly shaken." printed twice. Aborted aircraft (Nightjar 1 "Rough running, aborting" T+008) later shot down over target (T+047). "Bombs falling on Millbrook Aerodrome" — that's ours (home raid, ok?).
Fuel 882 and munitions 583 stockpiled — useless. Only supplies matter.
**W20-23 (Northern Approaches).** Nordwall fell in theater week 1, Grauburg in week 4 -> WAR OVER, VICTORY at W23. Never even flew the heavy bomber I researched. Seasoned is too easy once close support + feint is found.
END: claimed 105, reported 113, actually destroyed 41. Lost 37 aircraft. Declassified lethality: cockpit 32%, fuel 26%, engines 24%, wing roots 12%, nose 10%, tail 5%, fuselage 1%, outer wings 0%. My W2 Wald instinct (nose/cockpit/engines) was right; the ground-crew "more plate on the wings" advice was the trap. Great payoff screen.
But: now I know the table. If it's the same next campaign the armor puzzle is solved forever.
Oddity: end text "The enemy air arm is broken" when I killed 41 aircraft total and won by walking the Army forward.

## Marta (Wald, full war, 28 weeks, won all three theaters)

The strategic route works for the first time.
- In theater 3, four weeks of pure strikes moved the front from +4 to +19 and took a sector by bombing alone.
- Close support then hit the 15-point cap twice and broke the front.
- "Strike first, then push" is now the best way to play.

**Still open after this round**
- Crippling was impossible with medium bombers in theater 2: a rear airfield that only heavies could reach counted toward the average.
- Aircrew starved the wing while supplies piled up.
- The front at the threshold was opaque.
- Gun cameras seemed weak.

**Fixed after this round**
- Crippling now counts the works within two sectors of the front, and Intelligence lists the works that count.
- Crews can be bought from the Ministry.
- The Intelligence Section shows a band for the front.
- The Y-Service's reliability follows its record.
- The map's beliefs are averaged.
- Captured sites keep their real condition.
- A failed command stops a chained launch.
- The rest text is accurate.
- The war-over banner prints once.

## Jonas (Seasoned, 21 weeks, two stalemates)

He had his best moments yet:
- Shelford's obituary named the press-home request he had refused, the operation Shelford died on.
- Linley came back as a prisoner.
- DB-211's patch count.
- "5 sets of kit to pack."

**Still open after this round**
- A phantom CO was posted missing who never flew.
- Sayings were shared between leaders of the same type.
- COs who stayed home filed flight reports.
- "One more week" was said in the last week.
- Confidence was stuck at 100.
- Repeated scenes and last calls.
- The wing's collapse offered no choices.

**Fixed after this round**
- The phantom CO.
- Each leader has his own sayings.
- A CO who stays home debriefs the crews instead.
- An Ace needs kills.
- Last calls are unique within an operation.
- The break-off order comes from the right man.
- Scenes are deduplicated by template.
- Newcomers have unique names.
- Stalemates are readable and cost confidence.
- Gutted squadrons can be merged into another of their type.
- The adjutant warns early.
